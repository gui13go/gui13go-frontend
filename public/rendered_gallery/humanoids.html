Honda ASIMO (Pioneer Tribute)

Developed by Honda Motor Co. • Unveiled 2000
Historical Milestone (Retired 2022)

The legendary pioneer that proved humanoid walking was possible. Launched in 2000 after decades of secret research (E-series and P-series), ASIMO was the world's first humanoid capable of smooth dynamic walking (ZMP control), stair climbing, hopping on one leg, opening thermos bottles, and serving tea, paving the path for the entire modern humanoid revolution.

Core Innovations & Capabilities
  • Zero Moment Point (ZMP) Gait Control: Groundbreaking dynamic walking mathematics developed by Honda engineers.
  • Bipedal Running & Hopping: Could run at 9 km/h with both feet temporarily airborne in mid-stride.
  • Five-Fingered Mechanical Hands: Actuators in palm allowed opening screw-caps and pouring tea into cups.
  • Pedestrian Path Prediction: Detected approaching humans and proactively adjusted walking trajectory to avoid collisions.
Degrees of Freedom (DoF): 57 degrees of freedom (Final 2011 Version)
Physical Dimensions: 130 cm (4 ft 3 in) • 48 kg (106 lbs)
Payload & Speed: 0.5 kg per hand • 9 km/h (5.6 mph) running
Onboard Brain / AI Stack: Honda Autonomous Behavior Control System with posture stabilization
Actuation Architecture: Precision harmonic drive servo motors with ground reaction sensors
Perception & Sensory Suite: Dual stereo optical camera eyes, ultrasonic sensors, and floor laser
Primary Target Applications:
  • Pioneered all foundational humanoid balance, gait, and ZMP control theory
  • Global scientific ambassadorship and STEM education inspiration
  • Foundational research that directly catalyzed modern autonomous walking robotics
